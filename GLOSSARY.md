# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around buying courses and getting money back.

## Language

### People

**Admin**:
A Cadence staff member who runs the platform, and the only person who can approve or decline a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: The team (a Team is a buyer of seat licences), Instructor (for refund decisions)

### Purchases

**Personal Purchase**:
A learner buying a course for themselves, which gives them access to it.
_Avoid_: Individual purchase, Single purchase

**Team**:
A group, owned by one user, that buys Team Licences for other people.
_Avoid_: Admins, staff (for the people who decide Refund Requests)

**Team Licence**:
A purchase of a number of Seats in a course for a Team. Buying one doesn't give the buyer access to the course.
_Avoid_: Team purchase, Bulk purchase

**Seat**:
One place on a Team Licence, handed out as a coupon code. Claiming a Seat gives the person who claims it access to the course.
_Avoid_: Licence (for a single place), Coupon (for the concept)

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a Personal Purchase. An Admin approves or declines it, and the decision is final. A Personal Purchase can have at most one Refund Request, and a Team Licence can't have one.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request no Admin has decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has accepted. Approving it is what leads to a Refund.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has turned down, always with a Decline Reason. It has no Refund, and the learner can't raise another Refund Request for that purchase. Buying the course again is a new purchase, which can have its own Refund Request.

**Decline Reason**:
The Admin's explanation, shared with the learner, of why their Refund Request was declined.

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund an Admin has approved where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the learner.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.
