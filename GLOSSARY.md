# Cadence

An online course platform where learners buy courses and work through their lessons.

## Language

### People

**Learner**:
A person who buys courses and watches their lessons.
_Avoid_: Student, customer, buyer

**Admin**:
A Cadence staff member who runs the platform, including deciding Refunds.

### Refunds

**Refund**:
A learner's request to get back the amount they paid for a course, from the moment it is Requested until an Admin Approves or Declines it. An Approved Refund gives back what the learner actually paid and ends their access to the course.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, cancellation, cancel my purchase, Pending

**Refund Window**:
The fourteen calendar days after a purchase, including the whole of the fourteenth day, during which a learner may request a Refund.

**Chargeback**:
A payment dispute the learner raises with their bank or card issuer, outside Cadence. It is never another name for a Refund.
_Avoid_: using it for any request a learner makes through Cadence
